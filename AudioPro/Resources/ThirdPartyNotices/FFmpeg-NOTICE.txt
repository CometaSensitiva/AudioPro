FFmpeg
======

AudioPro includes unmodified FFmpeg command-line binaries, which it runs as a
separate process to merge, convert and compress audio and video:

  ffmpeg-binary-arm64   FFmpeg 8.0.1, static build by Martin Riedl
                        https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de
                        Licensed under the GNU GPL, version 2 or later.

  ffmpeg-binary-x86_64  FFmpeg N-121793-g1eb2cbd865, static build by
                        Helmut K. C. Tessarek
                        https://evermeet.cx/ffmpeg/
                        Licensed under the GNU GPL, version 3.

Both builds are configured with --enable-gpl (they include, among others,
x264 and x265), so the binaries are distributed under the GNU General Public
License. The full text of GPL version 3 is in FFmpeg-GPL-3.0.txt.

FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.
https://ffmpeg.org
